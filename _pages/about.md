I am pursuing my PhD in Computer Science at Carnegie Mellon University where I'm fortunate to be advised by Professor [Deva Ramanan](http://www.cs.cmu.edu/~deva/). My research interests include neural rendering, 3D perception, and distributed learning. Most of my recent work centers around efficiently building 3D representations at scale.

I'm spending this summer at [Reality Labs Research](https://about.meta.com/realitylabs/) under the guidance of Drs. [Christian Richardt](https://richardt.name/) and [Michael Zollhoefer](https://zollhoefer.com/) after previously interning at [Argo AI](https://www.argo.ai/). In a past life, I spent seven years at [Palantir Technologies](https://www.palantir.com/) in various leadership roles across business and product development. Before then, I received my BS and MS in Computer Science from Stanford University where I worked on the [Legion](https://legion.stanford.edu/) programming sytem under Professor [Alex Aiken](http://theory.stanford.edu/~aiken/) and explored semantic segmentation under Professor [Daphne Koller](https://ai.stanford.edu/~koller/).
